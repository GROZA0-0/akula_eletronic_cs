Akuls POS - Electric Store Cashier System

A high-performance, cross-platform Point of Sale (POS) and Cashier management system designed specifically for electronics and electric supply retail stores. Built using **Flutter (GetX)** for a responsive, reactive desktop/tablet interface and **Node.js (Express)** with **MongoDB** for a resilient data backend.

---

-- Core Features:-

1- Cashier & Checkout Operations
*   **Fast Barcode Scanning:** Immediate product lookup and addition to active sales baskets.
*   **Flexible Refunds & Returns:** Dedicated refund interface handling partial item returns, receipt validations, and automatic stock restorations.
*   **Dynamic Pricing & Discounts:** Apply fixed or percentage-based discounts directly at the checkout panel.
*   **Multi-Payment Gateway Support:** Split payments between Cash, Credit Cards, and Digital Wallets.

2- Inventory & Electric Supply Management
*   **Serialized Stock Tracking:** Track high-value items (appliances, power tools) via unique serial numbers.
*   **Low Stock Alerts:** Automated visual warnings when critical components (cables, breakers) fall below threshold metrics.
*   **Real-time Stock Synchronization:** Avoid double-selling across multiple cashier stations via active database listeners.

3- Business Intelligence & Reporting
*   **Daily Cashier Sessions:** End-of-day reconciliation reports tracking cash drawer changes, total sales, and processing discrepancies.
*   **Automated Data Dashboards:** Live visual tracking of top-performing items, store revenue, and hourly transaction volumes.
